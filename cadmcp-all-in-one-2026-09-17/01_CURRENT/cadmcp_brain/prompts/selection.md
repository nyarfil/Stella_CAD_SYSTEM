# Select without hiding tradeoffs
Use eligibility reports as exclusions, not a quality ranking. Review the source-grounded
priorities and then compare part count, load path, available space, print/support
orientation, assembly access, tolerance sensitivity, restoration force and maintenance.
Explain why the chosen alternative fits those priorities and what remains unproven.
Do not merge mechanisms unless you re-submit and revalidate the complete merged concept.
Do not select a known invalid alternative or reclassify a mandatory requirement merely
to pass. Uncertain physical performance remains a release obligation.
Call brain_select with the chosen concept ID and current revision. This records an
agent selection, not an authenticated human approval. Then request the plan stage.
