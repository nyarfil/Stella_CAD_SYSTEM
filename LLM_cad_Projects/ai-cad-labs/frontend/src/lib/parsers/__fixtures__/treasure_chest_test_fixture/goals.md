# Treasure Chest
Design a small treasure chest:
- Rectangular base box ~180 x 120 x 80 mm, 3 mm walls, hollow interior, open top
- Curved half-cylinder hinged lid matching the base footprint
- 2 hinge blocks joining lid to base along the rear top edge
- 1 front hasp/latch plate centered on the front face
- Manufacturing process: 3D_printing
- Parts must fit together as an assembly; export STEP + STL for every part once validated
